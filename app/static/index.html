PRODUCT BRIEF v1.0
THE PRODUCT PROBLEM

Different departments describe the same product differently. That inconsistency undermines search, reporting, supplier mapping and trust.

Our product goal is not to replace every source system. It is to establish a shared governed meaning for product identity, classification and specifications, with explicit accountabilities and stable consumer interfaces.

PRIORITISATION

Why ProductMaster comes first

Classification, ownership and data-quality acceptance must precede product comparisons. Otherwise, different categories, units and versions produce misleading decisions.

  • Business value and user adoption
  • Dependencies and delivery risk
  • Data quality and controlled changes
  • Security, lifecycle and source authority
MEASURING VALUE

Product outcomes

Use measurable product-quality indicators without claiming fictional savings or historical business results.

  • Completeness of accepted classifications
  • Technical-attribute validation coverage
  • Change-request lead time and rejection reasons
  • Source reconciliation and consumer adoption
